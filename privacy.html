Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 July 2026

This page explains what information JS Electrical collects through this website, why, and how it's used. It applies only to jselectrical.co.uk and the contact form on it.

Information we collect

When you submit the contact form, we collect the name, email address, phone number (if provided) and message you enter. We don't collect any other personal information automatically, and this site does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

How we use it

Submitted details are used only to respond to your enquiry, provide a quote, and arrange or carry out the work you've asked about. We don't sell or share your information with any third party for marketing purposes.

How submissions are handled

The contact form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form handling service, which forwards submissions to JS Electrical's email inbox. Formspree's own privacy policy governs how it handles data in transit; see formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Fonts

This site loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. Loading the font may share your IP address with Google as part of delivering the font files.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept only as long as needed to respond to you and complete any related work, after which they're deleted.

Your rights

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it at any time by emailing enquiries.jselectrical@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update date at the top of this page will be revised.
